Privacy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: Bullsy collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data stored

Bullsy only stores on your device: the first names or nicknames of the players you enter, their colours, the game in progress, the history of your games and your settings (theme, language, input mode). This information is never sent to a server.

What Bullsy does not do

  • No account, no sign-up, no email address requested
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics
  • No data shared with third parties
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera

Deleting your data

You can delete a player or clear the history from within the app. Uninstalling Bullsy erases all of its data from your device.

Children

Bullsy is suitable for all ages and collects no data, including from children.

Changes

If a future version adds an online feature (for example a leaderboard between friends), this page will be updated before its release and the app will ask for your consent.
